⚡ Get my real Duolingo XP

Shows your true Total XP (Math and Music included), not the smaller languages-only number.

Option A — one-click button

  1. Drag this green button up to your bookmarks bar:

    📊 My Duolingo XP
  2. Go to duolingo.com and log in.
  3. Click the 📊 My Duolingo XP bookmark. A box shows your real numbers.

Can't drag? Right-click your bookmarks bar → Add page → paste the code below as the URL.

Option B — paste it once

  1. Open duolingo.com and log in.
  2. Press F12, click Console. If it warns, type allow pasting and Enter.
  3. then paste in the console and press Enter. (This copies it without the javascript: prefix, which is what the console needs.)

Your password is only ever used on Duolingo's own site. This page and the bookmark never see it, and nothing is sent to GitHub or anyone else — the numbers are read from your own logged-in Duolingo tab and shown only to you.
